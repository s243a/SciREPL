SciREPL Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

Summary

SciREPL is designed to be a privacy-respecting application. All code execution happens locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

Local Data Storage

SciREPL stores the following data locally on your device using your browser's localStorage:

  • Your session history (code cells and their types)
  • Command history for input recall
  • Preferences (e.g. cell counter state)

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time via Menu > Clear History.

Network Requests & Third-Party CDNs

SciREPL bundles most of its rendering libraries (Plotly.js, KaTeX, marked.js) directly in the app to minimize network requests. However, the following resources are loaded from third-party CDNs:

  • Pyodide (Python runtime, ~25MB) — from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Loaded on first launch and cached locally by the app for subsequent use.
  • Ko-fi widget (support button in Help screen) — from storage.ko-fi.com

When these resources are fetched, the CDN providers may receive:

  • Your IP address
  • Your device's user agent string (browser/OS information)
  • Timestamps of requests
  • URLs of requested resources

This is standard HTTP behavior and is not under our control. Caching is used to minimize connections to CDN networks — after the initial download, Pyodide and its packages are served from the local cache.

jsDelivr's privacy policy is available at jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net.

Your Python code, session data, and exported files are never transmitted to any server.

Code Execution

All Python code you enter is executed locally on your device using Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly). No code is sent to any remote server for execution.

File Export

When you export a notebook (.ipynb), the file is created locally on your device. If you choose to share it (via the system share sheet), the destination is determined by your choice (e.g. local storage, email, cloud services). SciREPL does not control or monitor where you share exported files.

Analytics & Tracking

SciREPL does not include any analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or tracking mechanisms.

Children's Privacy

SciREPL does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app does not require account creation or collect personal information of any kind.

Future Changes

If additional language runtimes are added (e.g. Prolog, R), they may also be loaded from CDNs and cached locally, subject to the same metadata exposure described above. This policy will be updated to reflect any changes.
