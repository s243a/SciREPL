SciREPL Pro v1.4.0

This update makes code completion smarter in two ways you choose between: an optional code model that runs on your phone, and AI suggestions from your own provider that are reviewed before they are sent and capped in cost. It also refreshes the model lists, reorganizes the Completion screen, and adds control over Android's status bar. These are the changes since v1.3.0.

In testing This version is being prepared for the SciREPL tester group. The current public release is v1.3.0. Join the testers for access when the rollout begins.

A local code model on your phone

  • An optional small code model (Qwen2.5-Coder 0.5B) runs on your phone through a native llama.cpp engine and suggests the next word when the built-in suggestions are unsure.
  • It works offline. Suggestions are computed on the device, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Install it under Menu → Completion → Local model. Google Play delivers the engine and the model (about 400 MB) when you tap Install, like an app update; Delete removes them again.
  • It needs a 64-bit ARM (arm64) phone with at least 4 GB of memory. On other devices the Completion screen shows why it is unavailable.
  • The model is available only when SciREPL Pro is installed from Google Play. The sideload APKs do not contain it and cannot download it; the Completion screen says "this install cannot download it". Everything else in this release, including the built-in suggestions and AI suggestions through your own provider, works in sideloaded installs.

AI suggestions, reviewed and capped

  • Tap … at the end of the suggestion row, the …AI extra key, or Ctrl+Shift+Space for five alternatives at the cursor; tap again for five more. Nothing is sent until you ask.
  • Suggestions come from the AI Assistant's provider and model by default, or from a separate provider and model you choose for suggestions, using the API key you saved for it.
  • Each request is shown, exactly as it will be sent, before it goes. Choosing Always allow skips that review only for the same provider, endpoint and model; changing any of them asks again.
  • Daily and monthly spending caps apply (USD 0.25 a day and USD 5 a month by default). They fail closed: if a model's price is unknown, no request is sent.
  • Suggestions that could not start the next line are dropped, and accepting one adds a missing , or : in the same undoable edit.

Refreshed model lists

  • Fast, low-cost models come first in the suggestion picker, the agent picker hides lite models, and Show all models lists the rest.
  • The suggestion model can also be chosen in AI Settings.
  • Temperature now follows each model's catalogue entry, and GPT-6 Luna is the default model for direct OpenAI.

Completion knows your variables

  • Completion knows what your variables hold before a cell runs: after x = np.linspace(…), typing x. offers array attributes.
  • Menu → Completion is split into General, AI suggestions and Local model sections, so each kind of suggestion has its own settings.

Status bar and saved keys

  • Menu → Appearance → Hide Android status bar offers Never, In landscape, or Always.
  • Panels and dialogs keep the status-bar space. A status bar you swipe back stays for 5 seconds over a readable scrim, and its icons follow the app theme.
  • API keys are saved per provider under AI Settings → Saved keys, so switching provider never sends one provider's key to another.

The local model sends nothing off the device. AI suggestions go directly from your device to the provider you choose, with your own API key; the privacy policy lists exactly what a request contains.
