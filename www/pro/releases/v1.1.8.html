Android Pro · v1.1.8

SciREPL Pro v1.1.8

This update makes more room for your notebook on a phone, especially in landscape, and makes editing and navigation easier. These are the changes since v1.1.7.

More notebook space in landscape

  • In short landscape layouts, hide the new-cell panel and bring it back with the header control or an upward swipe from the bottom edge.
  • In wider landscape layouts with a workbook sidebar, tap the Sci REPL title to show or hide that sidebar when several workbooks are open.
  • The new-cell panel gets out of the way while you edit an existing cell. An optional Appearance setting hides the Android status bar in landscape; it returns in portrait.

Smoother touch editing

  • Double-tap a cell's displayed source, or use its edit button, to open the editor.
  • While editing, the header shows Apply and Cancel actions; they disappear when editing ends.
  • On Android, Back dismisses the keyboard first. If the cell remains in edit mode, another Back cancels the draft.

Clearer Help and controls

  • Help now has sections, including a UI guide for the landscape controls and cell-editing gestures. Its keyboard suggestions explain the trust trade-off of installing a third-party keyboard.
  • Search now has the same header-shortcut choices as Tour: Always, When there is room, or Never. Search remains available through Menu → Find in notebook when its header button is hidden.
  • The Japanese AI-setup Tour page now keeps its final controls clear of the Android navigation bar in portrait.

The landscape controls appear where screen height is limited; portrait and larger landscape layouts retain the usual workspace.
